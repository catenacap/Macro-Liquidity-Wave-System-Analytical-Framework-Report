A multi-factor macro-liquidity forecasting and market regime framework designed to model and anticipate:

- Bitcoin macro cycles
- Equity liquidity sensitivity
- Treasury refinancing pressure
- QE / Stealth QE transmission
- Global risk-on / risk-off transitions
- Debt-driven liquidity requirements
- Macro credit expansion cycles

# Overview

This framework combines:

- Global liquidity
- Shadow monetary base
- Stealth QE mechanics
- Yield Curve Control pressure
- China Credit Impulse
- Treasury debt rollover requirements
- MOVE volatility conditions
- Global risk appetite

into a unified probabilistic forecasting and cycle-detection engine.

The system attempts to answer:

> Where is liquidity flowing, how strong is it, and which assets are likely to respond next?

The framework treats:

- liquidity as the primary market fuel source,
- volatility as a suppressor/amplifier,
- debt refinancing as future liquidity demand,
- and BTC/equities as downstream liquidity expressions.

# MOVE Index Functionality

## Purpose

The MOVE Index acts as:

- volatility suppressor,
- liquidity effectiveness dampener.

---

## Behavioral Effect

### High MOVE

- suppresses liquidity transmission,
- reduces speculative responsiveness.

### Low MOVE

- amplifies liquidity effects,
- enhances risk-on transmission.

This is important because:

> Liquidity does not impact markets equally under all volatility regimes.
